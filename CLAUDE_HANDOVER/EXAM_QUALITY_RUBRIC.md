## Critical errors

A paper must remain UNRANKED if it has unresolved critical errors such as:

- Incorrect paper/theme division
- Incorrect total marks
- Missing mandatory examination components
- Materially incorrect answers
- Broken programming problems
- Incomplete markschemes
- Missing essential diagrams or data
- Failure to meet mandatory specimen-format requirements

Fix critical errors before ranking.
