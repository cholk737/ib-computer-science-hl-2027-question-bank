- Correct specimen-style structure

C. ANSWER AND MARKSCHEME CORRECTNESS — 25 POINTS

- Accurate solutions
- Correct calculations
- Correct Python programs
- Valid algorithmic reasoning
- Detailed partial-credit marking
- Alternative valid solutions
- Matching question and markscheme totals

D. SPECIMEN REALISM — 15 POINTS

- Appropriate IB command terms
- Similar reasoning depth
- Realistic question complexity
- Authentic assessment style
- Appropriate marking expectations
- Original questions that do not copy the specimen

E. TOPIC AND QUESTION-TYPE BALANCE — 10 POINTS

- Appropriate syllabus coverage
- Variety of question types
- Realistic difficulty distribution
- Genuine Python construction in Paper 2
- Appropriate application and reasoning
