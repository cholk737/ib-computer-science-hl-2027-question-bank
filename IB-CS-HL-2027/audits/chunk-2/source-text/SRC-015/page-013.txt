        the ground position of an object;
        It consists of three segments: satellites, control (made up of Earth-based monitor stations),
        and GPS receivers (user equipment);
        A number of satellites continuously send out signals containing their position and
        the exact time the signal was sent;
        GPS receiver (accept mobile phones) picks up the signal and determines the distance
        to each satellite (by calculating the time required to receive the signal);
        By using trilateration, it determines the exact location (by knowing the distance to at
        least four satellites);
