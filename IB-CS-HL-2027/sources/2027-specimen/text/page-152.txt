       Introduction

       The integration of technology in healthcare has transformed how medical services are delivered.
       However, with this increased use of technology, healthcare institutions like private hospitals have
       become attractive targets for cybercriminals and therefore face significant security challenges
 5     that could have devastating impacts. This case study highlights the interventions used to prevent
       security incidents by one private hospital, TaylorMade Healthcare, and explores some network
       security practices.

       Background

       TaylorMade Healthcare uses high-speed internet to operate its network infrastructure,
10     which includes computers, servers, switches, routers, wireless access points, and firewalls.
       These devices and systems are connected internally and to the internet, enabling staff to access
       medical records, communicate with other users and patients, exchange information, and transfer
       data. The devices and systems work cohesively and are vital for the hospital’s smooth operation,
       including patient care, data management, and resource handling.

15     TaylorMade Healthcare stores vast amounts of sensitive data, such as patient health records,
       financial information, and confidential documents, which it must safeguard to maintain trust and
       confidence among its patients, staff, and stakeholders.

       The hospital’s director acknowledges the importance of securing the network and data against
       risks like malware                                                                                                                                                                   infections, phishing                                                                                                                                                                                              attacks, and unauthorized access.        Bring-your-own-device (BYOD) policy

       TaylorMade Healthcare has adopted a bring-your-own-device (BYOD) policy that permits staff
       to use personal electronic devices for work tasks. Although a BYOD policy can provide many
25     benefits, such as increased flexibility and convenience, it also has several weaknesses.

       Ransomware and rootkit attacks

       TaylorMade Healthcare recently suffered a ransomware attack that targeted its network
       infrastructure. The attack was launched through a phishing email that appeared to be from
       a trusted source. When an unsuspecting staff member clicked on the link in the email,
30     the ransomware gained access to the hospital’s network and quickly spread, encrypting
       sensitive data and rendering it inaccessible.

       The consequences of the attack were severe, leading to significant data loss and system
       downtime. The ransomware attack encrypted patients’ data and prevented staff from
       accessing patient records for several days. This led to substantial disruptions.

35     TaylorMade Healthcare’s reputation has suffered due to the attack, with patients expressing
       concern about the security of their data. There was also a significant financial impact, with
       TaylorMade Healthcare paying the attackers a ransom to regain access to its encrypted data.

       Another notable concern is a rootkit attack, which uses malicious software to hide itself within
       an operating system to gain administrative-level access to the network. Rootkits are notoriously
40     difficult to remove.
