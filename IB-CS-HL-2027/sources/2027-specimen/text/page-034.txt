                          Candidates should discuss prevention and response, expressing their opinions on the
                          effectiveness of the strategies. The best responses will demonstrate research and cite real-
                          world related events and/or experts in the field.

                          Prevention
                          Implementing robust security protocols: TaylorMade Healthcare can establish stringent
                          security protocols, including firewalls, encryption, email filtering, and regular software
                          updates. These measures can prevent many common attacks, but candidates should be
                          aware that security measures quickly become outdated.

                          Access control and authentication: Implement strict access control policies, ensuring
                          employees have access only to the data necessary for their role. Use strong authentication
                          methods like two-factor authentication.

                          User awareness training: Educating staff about potential threats, such as phishing scams
                          and ransomware attacks, can prevent many attacks. Candidates should be aware that
                          human error remains a significant challenge. TaylorMade Healthcare should have clearly
                          defined procedures and protocols for new staff and departing staff. No matter how much
                          training is provided, it is virtually impossible to eliminate all human errors that might lead to a
                          security breach.

                          Network segmentation and access control: This strategy can limit the damage if a breach
                          does occur by preventing hackers from accessing the entire network. Candidates should be
                          aware that this strategy may not prevent the initial breach and can be complex to implement
                          and manage.

                          Update and patch management: Ensure all software, including operating systems and
                          applications, are kept up to date with the latest security patches.

                          Regular security audits and assessments: Conduct regular security audits and risk
                          assessments to identify and address vulnerabilities.

                          Monitoring and logging: Continuously monitor network activity and maintain logs to detect
                          and investigate suspicious activities.

                          Physical security: Enhance physical security measures to prevent unauthorized access to
                          servers and other critical IT infrastructure.

                          Vendor risk management: Assess and manage the cybersecurity risks associated with third-
                          party vendors and service providers.

                          Cybersecurity insurance: Consider purchasing cybersecurity insurance to mitigate financial
                          risks associated with data breaches.

                          Collaboration with law enforcement and cybersecurity agencies: Work with law enforcement
                          and cybersecurity agencies for threat intelligence sharing and guidance on emerging threats
                          and best practices.
