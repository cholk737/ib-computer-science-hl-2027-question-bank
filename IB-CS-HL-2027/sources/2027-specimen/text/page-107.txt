                            Steps to reduce potential risks
                            •                                               Implement robust security protocols: TaylorMade Healthcare can establish the following
                                       steps.
                            •                                               Security protocols: Ensure there is a failover system for firewalls, security software,
                                       email filtering, and regular software updates.
                            •                                               Device management: Implement a mobile device management (MDM) solution to control
                                       which devices can access company data, enforce security policies, and remotely wipe
                                       data if necessary.
                            •                                               VPN and secure connections: Ensure that all devices connecting to the company
                                       network do so through a secure connection like a VPN.
                            •                                               Regular audits: Periodically check and verify the security status of all devices that
                                       connect to the network.
                            •                                               Data segregation: Use applications that can segregate corporate data from personal
                                       data on the device.
                            •                                               User awareness training: Educating staff about potential threats, such as phishing
                                       scams and ransomware attacks, can prevent many attacks. Candidates should be aware
                                       that human error remains a significant challenge. TaylorMade Healthcare should have
                                       clearly defined procedures and protocols for new staff and departing staff, such as not
                                       connecting to open Wi-Fi networks and not downloading untrusted apps.
                            •                                               Employees should be made aware of the damage caused by malware.

                            Risks for bring your own device (BYOD) policy
                            •                                               Device diversity: A variety of device types, operating systems, and software versions can
                                       make standardized security measures challenging.
                            •                                               Uncontrolled environments: Personal devices might connect to insecure networks,
                                       increasing the risk of exposure to threats.
                            •                                               Data leakage: There is a risk of sensitive organizational data being stored alongside
                                       personal data and potentially being shared inadvertently.
                            •                                               Loss or theft: Personal devices can easily be lost or stolen, risking the exposure of any
                                       stored organizational data.
